My work sits at the intersection of **extended reality (XR)**, **human–computer interaction**, and **perceptual science**. Current focus areas are VR interaction, cybersickness, multisensory perception, and human–AI interaction.

I am joining the R2 Lab at UCF, working with [Dr. Rohith Venkatakrishnan](https://www.cs.ucf.edu/person/rohithvenkatakrishnan/) and [Dr. Roshan Venkatakrishnan](https://www.cs.ucf.edu/person/roshanvenkatakrishnan/).

### Research

- **VR locomotion & cybersickness** — How camera motion, speed, rotation, and trajectory shape comfort and sickness, toward design guidelines and adaptive control.
- **Multisensory conflicts** — How visual, vestibular, and proprioceptive cue conflicts affect balance, presence, and performance in immersive environments.
- **Human–AI interaction in VR** — Trust, adaptation, and mental models when people work with intelligent agents inside virtual environments.

### Selected work

- **[NeuroTransformer](https://raghavnanjappan.com/publications.html)** — Transformer model for EEG motor-imagery classification. ICDSA 2024; *Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems*, 2025.
- **[VR Cybersickness Camera Rig](https://raghavnanjappan.com/virtual-reality.html)** — Configurable Unity camera system for studying locomotion perturbations and user comfort.
- **[Get Cooking! VR](https://raghavnanjappan.com/virtual-reality.html)** — VR cooking tasks used to study everyday interaction techniques and performance.

### Currently

Building experimental VR platforms in Unity / C# to study locomotion, camera motion, and user comfort.
